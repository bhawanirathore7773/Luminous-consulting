Who we are

We started from a specific frustration: SAP engagements that either had deep technical skill with no business judgment, or strong business consulting with no ability to actually configure and build the system. This practice exists to close that gap — functional and technical depth under one roof, accountable to the same outcome.

Our philosophy

SAP engagements go wrong more often from unclear ownership than from any single technical mistake — a custom object no one will retire, an integration no one monitors, a role conflict no one reviews. We treat clarity of ownership as part of the deliverable, not an assumption someone else will handle.

We'd rather scope something narrow and finish it than propose something broad and stall. That shapes how we approach everything from a single ABAP fix to a multi-year transformation program.

How we work

Every engagement follows the same six-stage approach — discover, assess, design, deliver, stabilize, optimize — regardless of size. Small engagements move through it quickly; large ones spend more time in each stage, but the stages don't change.

Quality

Quality shows up in how work gets reviewed, not in a statement about caring how work gets reviewed. Configuration and code changes go through peer review before testing, test coverage is scoped against actual business processes rather than a generic checklist, and cutover plans are rehearsed before they're run for real.

Security

We work under the principle of least privilege — access to a client's systems is scoped to what a given engagement actually requires, time-limited where possible, and removed at the end of the engagement rather than left open. Client data and credentials are never used or stored outside the scope of the engagement they relate to.
